Frequently asked questions

How do I restore a purchase I already paid for?

Open Settings and tap any feature marked with the PRO badge. The Budgetra Pro screen opens — tap Restore Purchases at the bottom. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Open the iOS Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → Budgetra, and cancel there. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date, otherwise the next period is charged.

Where are my backups stored?

In your own iCloud Drive, never on our servers. You can find them in the Files app → iCloud Drive → Budgetra → Backups. iCloud Drive must be enabled on the device; if it is off, Budgetra will report that backup storage is unavailable.

I forgot my backup password. Can you recover it?

No, and this is by design. Backups are encrypted with a password only you know — we never receive it and it is not stored anywhere outside your device. Without that password the backup cannot be decrypted by anyone, including us.

How do I get my data out of the app?

Settings → Backups → Export to file creates a full backup file you can keep anywhere. Budgetra Pro additionally exports your data as CSV or PDF.

How do I delete my data?

All your financial data lives on your device, so deleting the app removes it. Backups are separate: delete them in the Files app → iCloud Drive → Budgetra → Backups.

Does Budgetra need an account or an internet connection?

Neither. The app works fully offline and there is nothing to sign up for. An internet connection is only used for optional exchange rates and for purchases.
